🏛️ 1. End-to-End Pipeline

Extractive, no text generation

A question is checked, embedded, matched against its own language's passages, reranked, and answered with the best span from the top passages. Every stage is timed and returned with the answer.

🧪 5. Design Choices

Why it is built this way
  • Extractive answers, no text generation: every answer is a sentence copied from a retrieved passage, with the extracted span in bold; the large reader takes about 43 ms and refuses when it isn't confident.
  • One multilingual embedding model: multilingual-e5-base covers Hindi, Marathi and English in one 768-dimensional space.
  • ONNX Runtime instead of PyTorch: the system only runs trained models. The switch was verified against the original: stored-vector cosine 0.99999 over 1,000 passages, and 224/224 identical answers.
  • Exact search instead of an approximate index: all 13M vectors fit in GPU memory, and scanning one language block takes about 15 ms, so results are exact.
  • Scale to zero, one container: no cost while idle; simultaneous requests queue on a single GPU instead of starting more.

⚠️ 8. Known Limitations

Measured, not hidden
  • Off-topic questions: a similarity-based gate cannot separate them; off-topic questions score 0.86–0.92, as high as answerable ones (median 0.88). This needs another signal, such as a cross-encoder or a classifier.
  • Unseen questions: on validation questions the answer overlaps the dataset answer only weakly (F1 8%); retrieval is strongest in English.
  • Cautious in Hindi and Marathi: the large reader refuses 23% of answerable Hindi and Marathi questions (6% in English) rather than guess; most of those refusals replace wrong answers.
  • First load: after 7 idle minutes the server stops, and the next visitor waits while 20 GB of vectors load.
